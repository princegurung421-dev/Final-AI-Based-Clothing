# WearWise — Features & Business Overview

WearWise is an online fashion store with a built-in personal styling assistant. It helps shoppers discover clothing they love, build outfits from items they already own, and check out smoothly — all in one place.

## What WearWise Offers Shoppers

### A Personal Styling Assistant
- A friendly chat assistant that helps customers find outfits, suggest looks, and answer style questions.
- Recommendations adjust to the local weather so suggestions feel right for the day.
- The assistant can look inside the store catalogue and also inside the customer's own saved wardrobe to mix and match pieces.

### Shop the Store
- Browse a curated catalogue of fashion products.
- View clear product pages with multiple photos, sizes, and stock availability.
- Read and write product reviews to help the community make better choices.
- Discover items on sale with clearly marked sale prices.

### My Wardrobe
- Customers can upload photos of clothes they already own and save them to a digital wardrobe.
- The styling assistant uses this wardrobe to suggest new outfits and recommend store items that complete the look.
- Encourages re-wearing existing clothes instead of always buying new — a sustainable angle.

### Shopping Cart & Checkout
- Add items to a cart in different sizes.
- Secure online payment by card.
- Promotional codes and discounts can be applied at checkout.
- Orders are saved to the customer's account so they can revisit their purchase history.

### Account & Profile
- Customers create an account to save their cart, wardrobe, orders, and preferences.
- Long-lasting sign-in so returning customers don't have to log in every visit.
- Profile management for personal details.
